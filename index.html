⚠️ Strike Information
Consequences & how to clear your strike
🚨 Consequences
📝
1 Strike or more
You cannot join the Summative Test
Your strike must be cleared before the exam date.
🚫
3 Strikes or more
You cannot join the class
You will be asked to leave until your strikes are resolved.
How to clear your strike
1
Write a text based on the current chapter Chapter 4 → Analytical Exposition · Chapter 5 → Narrative Text
2
Minimum 3 paragraphs, each with at least 5 sentences All text structure parts must be included.
3
Submit on paper directly to your teacher Handwritten or printed — your teacher will review it in class.
4
Your teacher will clear your strike once it's approved Check back here after submission to see it removed from the board.
