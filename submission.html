📄 Submission Format Guide
All tasks must be submitted as a PDF file. No photos, screenshots, or image files.
⛔ Do NOT submit JPG, PNG, or photo files — only PDF is accepted.
1
📷 Take a clear photo
Use your phone camera. Make sure the text is readable, well-lit, and not blurry or cropped.
2
🔍 Scan & extract text
Open Google Lens and tap on your photo. Select all the text, then copy it. Or use Microsoft Lens to scan directly.
3
📋 Paste into Google Docs
Go to docs.google.com, create a new document, paste your text and tidy up the formatting.
4
📥 Download as PDF
In Google Docs: File → Download → PDF Document (.pdf). Your file is ready!
📱 Recommended free tools
🔍 Google Lens 📷 Microsoft Lens 📋 Google Docs 🔁 iLovePDF ⚡ SmallPDF
