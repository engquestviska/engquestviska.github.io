CONCEPT · SEMESTER-ENDEngQuest Chronicle

Eight slides, the share card, and the records page. Press play on the phone to watch it the way a student would. Click any slide in the strip to replay its motion. Numbers are made up.

Watch itMoves on by itself every 4.5 seconds. Click the phone to skip ahead.

One look on every slide: a printed masthead at the top, thin rules, and one giant number cropped off the edge. Blue is used once per slide, on the thing that matters.

Transitions: when the background stays the same, the next slide glides in from the side. When it changes (paper to black, black to blue), the new color spreads out in a circle.

Motion: rules draw in, numbers count up, tiles and dots fill one by one, the student's own words scroll past, the score marker slides along the 0–100 scale, and the class rank counts down from 36 to the student's place.
