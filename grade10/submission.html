Guide

PDF Submission Format

Do not submit JPG, PNG, screenshots, or photo files. Only PDF files are accepted.
1
Take a clear photo

Use your phone camera. Make sure the text is readable, bright, and not cropped.

2
Scan and extract text

Use Google Lens or Microsoft Lens to scan your work and copy the text neatly.

3
Paste into Google Docs

Create a document, paste your text, then tidy spacing and formatting.

4
Download as PDF

In Google Docs, choose File, Download, then PDF Document.
